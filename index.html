Research

I’ve built AI for patients, clinicians and regulated industries, and seen where it fails. These are the questions I want to answer in a PhD.

  • When do warm, reassuring health chatbots give in to patients, and how does that lead people to trust them too much? Grounded in shipping LLM health features with human-in-the-loop checks at Gewardz Health, and evaluating LLM failure modes at Outlier.
  • Do the reasons AI agents give actually match how they make their decisions? Grounded in designing a production multi-agent system with safe handoffs and human approval points.
  • Can foundation models for medical images be trusted on data they weren’t trained on? Building on my MSc dissertation benchmarking SAM and Cellpose across microscopy datasets.
